Guess the Hour

New York complains about different things at different hours. From 11 PM to 3 AM, more than half of everything reported to 311 is noise. By 9 AM, noise is under a tenth and the line fills with parking, trash and broken apartments. Play six quick rounds, or explore the whole day on the dial.

    The data

    311 Service Requests from 2010 to Present, NYC Open Data. Every request created in 2025: 3,653,118 rows, counted by the hour in created_date and grouped into nine categories. Every question in the game is generated from these counts.

    How it was cleaned

    • 1,923 Health Department records stamped exactly 12:00:00 AM were removed. They carry a date but no real time.
    • Categories come from complaint_type and agency: types starting with “Noise”; Illegal Parking and Blocked Driveway; HEAT/HOT WATER; other housing (HPD) requests; Sanitation (DSNY); Transportation (DOT); Water System and Sewer; Encampment and Homeless Person Assistance.

    What it can't show

    • 311 counts who calls, not what happens. Neighborhoods that trust the city less report less.
    • Requests about unhoused people are filed by others, about them. They appear here only as citywide totals per hour.
    • The hour is when the request was filed, not when the problem began.
